About PlanMyStudy

Empowering Every Student to Succeed

PlanMyStudy was created to help students eliminate exam anxiety and plan their revision systematically.

🎯

Our Mission

Most students fail exams not because of a lack of effort, but due to disproportionate time allocation and cramming without revision buffers. PlanMyStudy combines mathematical scheduling with cognitive study science to keep every learner on track.

🔒

Privacy & Architecture

Built with Django and a dual-database architecture: PostgreSQL safeguards your schedules, FAQs, and support tickets with strict transactional guarantees, while MongoDB Atlas asynchronously logs conversational feedback.

Direct Support Channel

Have a question or need assistance?

You don't need to wait for email queues. Our integrated AI Support Assistant is online 24/7 on every page. If your query requires specialized human attention, the assistant will automatically generate a support ticket for our staff.
